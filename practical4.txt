PRACTICAL 4 – STRACE SYSTEM CALL ANALYSIS

Command:
strace cat sample.txt

System Calls and Kernel Services:

1. execve()
   Executes the cat program.

2. openat()
   Opens sample.txt for reading.

3. read()
   Reads the contents of sample.txt from the file.

4. write()
   Writes the contents to standard output (screen).

5. close()
   Closes the opened file.

6. exit_group()
   Terminates the cat process.

Sequence:
execve() → openat() → read() → write() → read() → close() → exit_group()
